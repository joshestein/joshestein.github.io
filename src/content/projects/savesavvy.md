[SaveSavvy](https://savesavvy.co.za/) is a mobile app I built to help me track
my finances and gain understanding into where I'm spending my money. Do you know
how much you spend each month on groceries?

It allows you to upload bank statements. These are automatically parsed and
categorised. You can view your spending habits over time, and get insights into
where your money is going.

There are also some budgeting features.

The biggest wins for me are (1) getting an overview from all my different
accounts and (2) being able to see into which categories I'm spending the most
money.
