I can't believe it's 2026 and there isn't a way to schedule a WhatsApp message.

For my new job I've been writing a lot of messages to people. Sometimes it's the
evening, sometimes it's the weekend. I want to schedule a message for somebody
during working hours. I'm _trying_ to be considerate and not send messages at
3am.

Totally reasonable, right? And should just exist? Can't believe it doesn't.

Spent a weekend hacking this together.

It lives here: https://github.com/joshestein/whatsapp-scheduler
