I love yoga. I go to a few different studios. But I hate the loading times of
the websites, and I hate having to visit all the different websites to see
what's available. So I built a personal dashboard to consolidate all the
information I need. I really think this represents what the future of software
engineering is going to look like. Highly personalised, bespoke services that
cater to what you want and what you need.

Here's my dashboard: https://joshstein.io/yoga
